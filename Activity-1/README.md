## ♡ ACTIVITY OVERVIEW

This activity focused on applying the seven basic principles of presentation design: visual hierarchy, contrast, balance, alignment, proximity, consistency, and simplicity. I created a promotional poster for a Free Coffee event using appropriate colors, typography, images, and layout to make the design attractive, organized, and easy to understand.

## ♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned how to apply the seven principles of design to create an effective and visually appealing poster. I improved my skills in choosing color combinations, arranging text and images, and organizing information through proper layout and visual hierarchy.

One challenge I encountered was positioning the text and images without making the poster look crowded. I solved this by adjusting their placement and organizing related information together. I also learned that a good design should not only look attractive but should communicate its message clearly.

In the future, I can apply these principles when creating presentations, posters, infographics, and other digital materials. I will continue improving my creativity and design skills by exploring different layouts, color combinations, and typography styles.
