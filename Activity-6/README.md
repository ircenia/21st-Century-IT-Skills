## ♡ ACTIVITY OVERVIEW

This activity focused on creating a one-page business website using an AI-powered website-building tool called **Lovable.dev**. For this activity, I developed **Wear Pretty**, a women's clothing brand that features stylish, comfortable, and affordable fashion.

The website showcases the brand's identity, featured clothing collections, brand story, lookbook, customer reviews, newsletter signup, and contact information. I used a feminine and modern design with soft colors, elegant typography, and organized sections to create an inviting online shopping experience.

## ♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned how to transform a business idea into a functional and visually appealing website using Artificial Intelligence. I gained experience in using Lovable.dev and discovered how written prompts can guide AI in generating website layouts, selecting design elements, and organizing content. I also learned the importance of maintaining a consistent brand identity through colors, typography, images, and overall presentation.

One challenge I encountered was making sure that the website reflected the identity of Wear Pretty while keeping its sections organized and easy to navigate. I addressed this by providing clear instructions, reviewing the generated design, and making adjustments to improve its appearance and functionality.

In the future, I can apply these skills when creating personal websites, online businesses, and other digital projects. This activity taught me that AI can make website development easier, but creativity, proper planning, and critical evaluation are still important in producing a quality website.
