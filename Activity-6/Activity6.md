Welcome to my Activity 06 portfolio for GE 4120. This activity focuses on creating a **one-page business website** using Artificial Intelligence and website-building tools.

For this activity, I developed **Wear Pretty**, a women's clothing brand with a feminine, modern, and elegant identity. Using **Lovable.dev**, I transformed my business concept into a responsive website that showcases clothing collections, introduces the brand, displays customer reviews, and provides shopping and contact features.

The website combines soft colors, elegant typography, fashion imagery, and organized content to create an inviting online shopping experience.

## 01 · Wear Pretty Boutique

**Wear Pretty** is a women's clothing brand that focuses on stylish, comfortable, and affordable fashion. Its concept is built around helping women feel confident through clothing that combines quality, style, and everyday comfort.

The website introduces visitors to the brand, showcases its featured clothing pieces, and encourages customers to explore and purchase products.

**Brand identity:**

- **Brand Name:** Wear Pretty
- **Business Type:** Women's Clothing Boutique
- **Style:** Feminine, modern, and elegant
- **Main Colors:** Blush Pink, Cream, White, and Gold
- **Target Audience:** Women who appreciate stylish and comfortable fashion

**Brand tagline:**

*Wear Pretty. Feel Confident.*

## 02 · Objective of the Website

The objective of the Wear Pretty website is to introduce new visitors to the brand and turn them into customers and loyal followers.

With a clean and feminine design, the website showcases featured clothing pieces, tells the brand's story, and builds customer trust through reviews and a lookbook.

It also aims to:

- Create a memorable online identity that reflects the Wear Pretty style and personality.
- Provide shoppers with an easy, mobile-friendly way to browse the collection.
- Encourage first-time purchases through a welcome discount and simple ordering.
- Turn one-time visitors into repeat customers through email updates and social media links.
- Explore how AI tools can transform a business idea into a professional website.

## 03 · Lovable.dev

**Lovable.dev** is an AI-powered website-building platform that helps users create websites through written instructions and prompts.

For this activity, I used Lovable.dev to develop the Wear Pretty website. I provided a detailed prompt describing the business concept, visual style, website sections, colors, typography, and desired features.

The AI then generated the website based on my instructions, allowing me to preview the design and make adjustments to the overall layout and presentation.

Using this tool helped me understand how Artificial Intelligence can simplify website development and turn creative ideas into working digital products.

## 04 · Features of the Wear Pretty Website

The website was designed as a complete one-page boutique website with different sections that introduce the brand and encourage customers to explore its collection.

### 01 · Homepage and Hero Section

The homepage welcomes visitors with the brand name, the headline **"Wear Pretty. Feel Confident."**, fashion imagery, and shopping buttons.

It also includes a navigation bar that allows visitors to explore different website sections.

### 02 · Featured Collection

The collection section displays six featured clothing products with product images, names, prices, and Buy Now buttons.

The featured products include:

- Blush Satin Slip Dress
- Golden Button Cardigan
- Puff Sleeve Linen Blouse
- Wide-Leg Belted Trousers
- Rosy Floral Wrap Dress
- Pink Power Blazer

### 03 · About the Brand

This section introduces the Wear Pretty story and its philosophy of creating stylish clothing that makes women feel confident.

It also highlights three brand values:

- Quality Fabric
- Affordable Style
- Made for Every Woman

### 04 · Lookbook

The lookbook features a gallery of fashion images showcasing different outfits and styling inspirations.

It provides visitors with a visual presentation of the brand's fashion identity.

### 05 · Customer Reviews

The testimonial section displays three customer reviews with star ratings to build trust and provide social proof.

### 06 · Newsletter Signup

The newsletter section encourages visitors to subscribe and receive a **10% discount on their first order**.

### 07 · Contact and Social Media

The contact section includes a contact form, email information, and social media icons for Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

The website also includes a footer with the brand identity and copyright information.

## 05 · Color Palette and Typography

The website uses a soft and feminine color palette to reflect the personality of Wear Pretty.

| Design Element | Application |
|---|---|
| Blush Pink | Backgrounds and highlighted sections |
| Cream | Main website backgrounds |
| White | Product cards and content areas |
| Gold | Buttons and decorative accents |

**Typography**

- **Playfair Display:** Used for headings to create an elegant and sophisticated appearance.
- **Poppins:** Used for body text to maintain readability and a clean, modern look.

The combination of these colors and fonts creates a consistent brand identity throughout the website.
