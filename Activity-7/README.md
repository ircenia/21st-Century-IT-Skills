## ♡ ACTIVITY OVERVIEW

This activity focused on generating a professional one-page resume using two Artificial Intelligence tools: **Claude AI and ChatGPT**. I provided both tools with the same personal information, educational background, clinical experiences, skills, and other qualifications to generate a resume suitable for an entry-level nursing application.

The activity also involved comparing the two AI-generated outputs based on their structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. Through this comparison, I was able to identify their strengths and weaknesses and determine how AI can help create a professional resume.

## ♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned how different AI tools can produce different results even when given the same instructions. I discovered that Claude AI provided a more detailed and organized resume, while ChatGPT produced a cleaner and more minimalist design. Comparing both outputs helped me understand the importance of proper formatting, clarity, and organization when creating a professional resume.

One challenge I encountered was deciding which resume format best presented my qualifications without making the information look too crowded or incomplete. I addressed this by comparing the strengths and weaknesses of both outputs and choosing a layout that highlighted my clinical experiences, educational background, and nursing skills while maintaining a professional appearance.

In the future, I can apply what I learned when updating my resume and preparing for job applications, especially as a future registered nurse. I will continue improving my skills in using AI tools by providing clear instructions, reviewing generated content, and making necessary revisions. This activity also taught me that AI can be a helpful resource, but personal judgment and decision-making are still important in producing quality work.
