Welcome to my Activity 07 portfolio for GE 4120. This activity focuses on **generating a professional resume using Artificial Intelligence tools**.

For this activity, I used two different Large Language Models (LLMs), **Claude AI and ChatGPT**, to generate a professional one-page resume using the same personal information and instructions.

The main purpose was to compare how different AI tools organize information, present qualifications, and create professional resume layouts. Through this process, I was able to evaluate their strengths and weaknesses and determine which output best represents my background and qualifications as a nursing student.

## 03 · Creating My Professional Resume

For this activity, I provided both AI tools with the same personal information and instructions to generate a professional one-page resume suitable for an entry-level nursing application.

The information included:

- Personal and contact information
- Professional summary
- Clinical experience and core responsibilities
- Leadership experience
- Clinical rotations
- Educational background
- Academic honors and awards
- Seminars and trainings
- Membership and volunteer experience
- Clinical skills and personal qualities
- Personal interests

I instructed both AI tools to use a **clean, modern, and minimalist format**, with clear section headings, concise information, and a professional appearance.

## 04 · Comparing Claude AI and ChatGPT

After generating the resumes, I compared the two outputs based on their structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.

| Criteria | Claude AI | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | More detailed sections with separate clinical rotations and experiences | Fewer, combined sections |
| Clarity | Organized bullet points and easy-to-identify information | Longer paragraphs and denser information |
| Formatting | Navy headings, underlines, and two-column skills section | Dark headings, thin lines, and teal subtitle |
| Overall Quality | More complete and detailed | Cleaner but less detailed |

### ✦ Claude AI Output

Claude AI produced a more comprehensive resume with clearly separated sections. Each hospital rotation was listed individually, making the clinical experiences easier to identify.

Its use of bullet points, bold hospital names, and organized sections made the resume easier to scan and understand.

### ✦ ChatGPT Output

ChatGPT generated a more minimalist resume with fewer combined sections. It used a simpler presentation with a focus on concise information.

However, some details were presented in longer paragraphs, making certain qualifications and clinical experiences less noticeable.

### ✦ My Evaluation

After comparing both outputs, I found that **Claude AI provided a more complete and detailed resume**, while ChatGPT offered a cleaner and more minimalist design.

For my final resume, I prioritized a professional and organized layout while retaining the most relevant information, especially my clinical experiences, skills, and educational background as a nursing student.
