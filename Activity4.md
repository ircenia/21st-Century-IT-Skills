This activity helped me appreciate the value of digital tools in education. I learned that presenting information effectively involves more than simply putting content on a page. It also requires organizing ideas, choosing an appropriate format, and considering how the audience will understand the material.

Collaborating on a group output also highlights the importance of communication, cooperation, and shared responsibility. Digital platforms make it easier to exchange ideas and work toward a common goal.

Overall, this activity strengthened my appreciation for technology as a learning resource and encouraged me to use digital tools more creatively and responsibly in future academic tasks.
