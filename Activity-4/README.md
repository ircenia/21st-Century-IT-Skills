## ♡ WHAT HAVE I LEARNED?

Through this activity, I learned how digital tools can make learning and collaboration easier. I improved my understanding of organizing information, presenting ideas clearly, and using online platforms to create and share learning materials.

One challenge I encountered was combining ideas and coordinating tasks with group members to complete our outputs. We addressed this through communication, cooperation, and sharing responsibilities. I also learned that interactive quizzes can make learning more engaging by allowing students to test their understanding.

In the future, I can apply these skills when preparing academic presentations, collaborating on group projects, and creating interactive learning materials. I will continue improving my digital skills by exploring different tools and using them effectively in my studies.
