Welcome to my **Activity 04 portfolio for GE 4120!**

This activity showcases our Group 5 outputs, including a group presentation and an interactive quiz. It highlights how digital tools can support collaboration, organize information, and make learning more engaging and accessible.

Through this activity, I learned how digital platforms can help students share ideas, work together, and present information effectively. The presentation allows learners to understand the topic through organized content, while the quiz provides an opportunity to review and test their knowledge.

Through this activity, I learned the importance of using digital tools to make learning and collaboration easier. I realized that creating an effective presentation requires proper organization, clear communication, and thoughtful presentation of information.

One challenge in group activities is combining different ideas and making sure that everyone contributes to the final output. This can be addressed through proper communication, cooperation, and sharing responsibilities among group members.

I also learned that interactive quizzes can make learning more engaging because they allow students to assess their knowledge and remember important concepts. In the future, I can apply these skills by using digital platforms more effectively for academic presentations, group projects, and other learning activities.
